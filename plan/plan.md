# Arcturus Premium Website Redesign Plan

Arcturus Professional Services will be presented as a premium, multi-page freelance consultancy website with clear written-enquiry journeys and strong search visibility.
The redesign will combine Big 4-style editorial confidence with a more personal first-person voice, distinctive Arcturus branding, and restrained modern motion.

## Who it's for

- Founders, consultants, small teams, and established businesses in Ireland, the UK, and wider Europe
- Businesses seeking LinkedIn management, email outreach, business support, lead generation, AI video creation, or email and DNS setup
- Decision-makers who prefer transparent pricing, documented processes, written communication, and direct freelancer access
- Search engines and AI assistants that need complete, factual, citation-ready HTML content

## Core features and experience

- Separate pages for Home, Services, all six services, About, How I Work, Contact, Privacy Policy, and Terms
- Service order used consistently throughout the website: LinkedIn Management, Email Outreach, Business Support, Lead Generation, AI Video Creation, Email Setup
- One clear H1 per page, short readable sections, factual pricing, reporting details, FAQs, related-service links, and service breadcrumbs
- First-person singular copy using “I”, with no “we”, “our”, or em dashes
- Written communication only, with no phone-call or call-booking buttons
- Contact form for name, email, company, service, and message, delivered to khushboo@arcturusprofessional.com
- Direct email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and Instagram contact options
- Black supplied logo used unchanged on ivory and other light surfaces
- White supplied logo used unchanged on navy and other dark surfaces
- Hemicube used for headings and Alesand Extra Bold used for prices, labels, and highlighted facts
- A clean, highly readable sans serif used for longer paragraphs and form content
- Generic non-branded icons only, with no third-party platform logos
- Complete SEO for every route in crawler-readable HTML, including unique titles, descriptions, canonicals, Open Graph tags, Twitter tags, and relevant JSON-LD
- Service and FAQ schema on service pages, Organization and Person schema on Home and About, and ContactPage schema on Contact
- Sitemap, robots.txt, and llms.txt covering all public pages and business facts
- Descriptive image alt text, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, strong contrast, and fully responsive layouts

## User flow

1. A visitor lands on Home and immediately understands who Khushboo supports, what services are available, and that communication is written only.
2. The visitor opens the Services menu or a service card and reaches a dedicated service page.
3. The service page gives a plain-language summary, inclusions, intended audience, process, pricing, reporting, FAQs, and related services.
4. The visitor follows a related-service link when the brief requires supporting work such as Lead Generation or Email Setup.
5. The visitor opens Contact and sends the written enquiry form, a prefilled email, or a WhatsApp message.
6. The site confirms successful form delivery and keeps privacy and service terms easily accessible from the footer.

## UI/UX feel

- Editorial corporate premium, inspired by the polish and confidence of leading Big 4 advisory websites without copying a specific firm
- Warm ivory and cream as the main canvas, deep navy as the dominant authority colour, and solid cobalt, muted lavender, and muted teal accents
- No gradients, neon colour, childish illustration, or generic equal-card template styling
- Layered rounded panels, soft realistic shadows, arches, offset geometry, generous whitespace, and balanced asymmetric compositions
- Large Hemicube headlines paired with restrained Alesand labels and pricing, while body copy remains spacious and easy to scan
- Large pricing blocks in deep navy so each service cost is immediately visible as real text
- Smooth page entry transitions, staggered section reveals, subtle panel lift, controlled shadow movement, button feedback, animated dropdowns, and polished mobile-menu motion
- Motion will remain refined rather than theatrical, with short durations and consistent easing
- Hover effects will use targeted colour, transform, border, and shadow transitions rather than broad animation of every property
- Reduced-motion preferences will remove non-essential movement while preserving usability and hierarchy
- Logo motion will be limited to subtle container or reveal effects; the supplied artwork itself will not be distorted, recoloured, redrawn, or animated internally
- Every page will receive a visual review at desktop and mobile widths so typography, spacing, overflow, buttons, transitions, and content hierarchy remain consistent

# Arcturus Premium Visual Redesign

Arcturus Professional Services will receive a complete visual redesign while every existing word, price, service, link, URL and SEO statement remains unchanged.
The result will present Khushboo Tomar as a premium independent digital professional through art-directed typography, colour, motion and interaction rather than a generic freelancer template.

## Who it's for

- Founders, consultants, small teams and established businesses in Ireland, the UK and wider Europe
- Decision-makers looking for direct access to an independent freelancer rather than an agency
- Visitors who value clear written communication, visible pricing and modern digital capability
- International visitors using desktop, laptop, tablet or mobile devices
- Visitors who prefer reduced motion or rely on keyboard navigation and accessible focus states

## Core features and experience

- Preserve every existing page, navigation label, service name, price, paragraph, FAQ, contact detail, link, URL and SEO statement exactly as it is now
- Rebuild the complete visual system so the difference is immediately obvious across Home, Services, all service pages, About, How I Work, Contact, Privacy, Terms and utility pages
- Retain Hemicube as a brand display font only for letters and symbols that render cleanly
- Use Sora Variable selectively for numerals and any specific Hemicube glyph that is missing, malformed or contains visible font artefacts
- Visually inspect every heading, price, date, label and number on every page so no broken or inconsistent glyph remains
- Keep Alesand Extra Bold for prices, compact labels and highlighted facts
- Keep a highly readable sans serif for paragraphs, forms, FAQs and longer content
- Replace the stock-photo hero treatment with an original visual atmosphere made from typography, translucent shapes, soft gradients, fine lines, nodes, grids and restrained motion
- Introduce a cohesive palette of warm ivory, deep navy, muted cobalt, soft violet, lavender, subtle teal and rich dark neutrals
- Use light and dark sections strategically, with soft glow, layered depth and controlled contrast
- Create varied editorial layouts instead of repeating heading, paragraph and equal-card patterns
- Give every service a distinct abstract visual motif while preserving its exact content
- Redesign navigation, buttons, cards, forms, breadcrumbs, pricing panels, contact controls and footer as one premium interaction system
- Retain official WhatsApp, LinkedIn and Instagram icon shapes and recognisable brand colours, including the Instagram gradient
- Strengthen hover, focus and active states with controlled lift, scale, colour, ring and glow feedback
- Add page-load reveals, staggered section entrances, kinetic headline moments, floating geometry, subtle parallax and refined section transitions
- Keep motion transform- and opacity-led where possible, avoid layout shifts and provide a complete reduced-motion treatment
- Preserve the existing black and white Arcturus logo artwork without recolouring, redrawing, distorting or animating the artwork internally
- Keep worldwide access unrestricted, with no country blocking or access-denied experience
- Add a small premium analytics element near the footer showing “Analytics unavailable”
- Open an elegant compact analytics panel from that element showing “Country data unavailable” rather than invented visitor figures
- Keep the analytics presentation secondary to contact and service actions
- Add no tracking, cookies, IP collection, country lookup or analytics provider during this visual-only phase
- Preserve the current crawler access, structured data, sitemap, robots rules and internal links without rewriting SEO content

## User flow

1. A visitor arrives on the existing Home page content, now presented through an oversized editorial hero and original animated visual composition.
2. The visitor uses the redesigned translucent navigation or service cards to move through the existing page structure.
3. Each service page presents the existing summary, price, inclusions, process, reporting, FAQs and related links in a visually distinctive service-specific composition.
4. Hover, focus and scroll motion guide attention without obscuring or delaying access to information.
5. The visitor reaches Contact through the existing links, floating contact controls or service actions and uses the existing written-enquiry options.
6. Near the footer, the visitor can open the understated analytics element and sees an honest unavailable state because no real analytics source is connected.
7. Mobile visitors receive a deliberately recomposed layout with clean spacing, compact motion and no horizontal overflow.

## UI/UX feel

- Premium personal brand rather than agency, SaaS or corporate consultancy
- Editorial, creative, slightly futuristic and internationally polished
- Expressive display typography with deliberate line breaks, fluid sizing and strong contrast between headline, label, price and body roles
- Hemicube used selectively as a recognisable brand accent, with Sora replacing only problematic letters, punctuation or numerals
- Warm ivory canvas balanced by deep navy full-width sections and carefully limited cobalt, violet and teal accents
- Sophisticated gradients, translucent layers, soft blur, fine digital grids, connection nodes and organic geometry used as recurring motifs
- Asymmetric compositions, overlapping panels, offset grids, full-bleed sections and controlled whitespace
- Distinct service visuals: network nodes for LinkedIn, message paths for Email Outreach, workflow elements for Business Support, structured data grids for Lead Generation, motion frames for AI Video Creation and network configuration layers for Email Setup
- Premium tactile buttons with clear focus rings, controlled glow, arrow movement and subtle press states
- Cards with thin borders, selective translucency, restrained shadows and varied silhouettes rather than six identical containers
- Smooth cinematic motion with consistent timing and easing, never bouncy, cartoonish or continuously distracting
- Dark footer with subtle digital lines, small nodes, logo treatment and colourful official social icons
- Excellent contrast, keyboard access, responsive hierarchy and a visually equivalent reduced-motion experience

## Implementation phases

### Phase 1: Complete visual redesign, built now

- Replace the current visual system across every existing page without changing any content or URL
- Apply the Hemicube and Sora glyph strategy and visually inspect every page for broken letters, numbers and punctuation
- Rebuild the hero without stock photography using original typography, abstract graphics, gradients, depth and motion
- Redesign navigation, all sections, service presentations, pricing blocks, cards, forms, buttons, icons, breadcrumbs, contact dock, analytics unavailable state and footer
- Add service-specific abstract motifs and varied editorial layouts
- Add restrained page, section, headline, card, button, icon and background motion
- Add intentional desktop, tablet and mobile compositions with reduced-motion support
- Preserve all existing functionality, worldwide access, crawler behavior, content, links, SEO text and contact destinations
- Complete a visual review of every page at desktop, tablet and mobile widths, including font rendering, spacing, contrast, hover, focus, motion and overflow

### Phase 2: Real privacy-conscious analytics, not built now

- Select and connect an approved analytics provider
- Confirm whether consent or a cookie notice is required for the chosen setup
- Replace unavailable states with real aggregated visitors-today, visitors-this-week and country-level totals
- Keep raw IP addresses, personal information and individual histories private

### Phase 3: Content and SEO refinement, not built now

- Review copywriting, page content and SEO text as a separate approved project
- Add only supplied and verified testimonials, case studies, statistics or business claims
- Refine information architecture or regional content only when content changes are explicitly approved

## Assumptions

- Every current text string, page, service name, price, FAQ, business detail, link, contact method and URL is approved and must remain byte-for-byte unchanged during the redesign
- Current SEO titles, descriptions, schema content, sitemap entries, robots rules and crawler files remain unchanged
- Hemicube is not removed completely; it remains on visually verified clean glyphs only
- Sora Variable is the approved fallback for all numerals and any individual Hemicube glyph that renders incorrectly
- Alesand Extra Bold remains reserved for prices, labels and compact highlights
- The existing readable sans serif remains appropriate for long-form text and forms
- Existing logo files are final and remain visually unchanged
- Existing social and contact destinations remain accurate
- The hero stock image can be removed because this is a visual change and does not alter website content
- Abstract graphics and motion will be created without adding large video assets or heavy 3D libraries
- Sophisticated gradients are approved for backgrounds, glow and the official Instagram treatment
- No visitor or country number will be displayed without a connected real data source
- The analytics element will show unavailable states only and will not collect data in Phase 1
- No analytics scripts, cookies, tracking pixels or consent banner will be added in Phase 1
- Worldwide public access remains enabled with no geography-based restrictions
- Existing contact-form delivery and all current interactions remain functional beneath the redesigned presentation
- Desktop receives the richest motion treatment; tablet and mobile retain the same visual identity with reduced decorative density for performance and clarity
- Reduced-motion preferences disable non-essential animation while preserving hierarchy and usability
